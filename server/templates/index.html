How to Play

🐜 The Game

Ants is a real-time strategy game where you program a bot to control a colony of ants. Your ants explore a toroidal (wrapping) map, gather food to spawn new ants, and battle enemy colonies. The goal is to raze enemy hills while protecting your own.

🗺️ The Map

Maps are grid-based and wrap around (toroidal). Cells are either land (passable) or water (impassable). Each player starts with one or more hills (spawn points). Food spawns periodically on the map.

⚔️ Combat

Combat is resolved simultaneously. When enemy ants are within attack radius of each other, the ant with more enemies nearby dies. If tied, all involved ants die. There's no randomness — combat is purely deterministic.

🏆 Scoring

• +2 points for razing an enemy hill
• −1 point when your hill is razed
• Bonus: if you're the last player alive, you get +2 for each remaining un-razed enemy hill
• Highest score wins. ELO ratings are updated after each match.

⏱️ Game End

Games end when: max turns reached (1000), one player eliminates all others, rank stabilized (the finishing positions can no longer change regardless of future play), or food stops being gathered. Matches range from 2 to 10 players, with 2-4 player games being most common.

🤖 Your Bot

Each turn, your bot receives the game state (visible ants, food, hills, water) and must output moves (N/S/E/W) for each ant within the time limit (1000ms). Ants that don't move stay in place. If your bot times out or crashes, your ants stop moving. Load time is 3000ms for initial setup.

  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
  -F "bot_name=MyBot" \
  -F "language=python|cpp|java|go|javascript|ruby|csharp" \
  -F "file=@mybot.zip"
